# Mehrooz Social Media

Short-form social videos (1080x1920 reels) for Permanent SEO. Each video lives in its own folder
(e.g. `semantic-seo-video/`) with `animation.html` (canvas animation, `draw(t)`), `make_audio.py`
(synthesized soundtrack) and `render.js` (Playwright + ffmpeg → `output/*.mp4`).

## Branding rules (apply to every video)

- Top-left: Permanent SEO logo (`assets/permanent-seo-logo.png`) with "PERMANENT SEO" / "SEO AGENCY".
- Bottom-right creator credit must read:
  - **M Mehrooz Shafique**
  - **Co-Founder of Permanent SEO**
- Closing scene: Permanent SEO logo + "Follow for more SEO tips" call to action.
