# Mehrooz Social Media: video and post style guide

Every video and post made in this repo follows this style unless the user says otherwise.
It's based on a reference reel the user shared (a ~23s vertical explainer about "6 AI agents" for SEO).
Copy the style, not the script: write an original script for every topic.

## Guiding voice (voiceover)

- **Every video has a guiding voiceover.** One narrator talks straight to the viewer and walks them through it.
- Tone: calm, confident and conversational, like a friend explaining something they built. Not hype and not salesy.
- Use short, punchy sentences of 2 to 8 words, with a natural pause between lines.
- Talk to the viewer as "you"/"your" about the problem, then switch to "I" for the solution ("So I built...").
- Pace: about 2.5 words per second. A full reel runs 15 to 30 seconds.
- Use a clear, warm voice (male or female) from a TTS or voice library.
  Do not clone or imitate a real creator's voice.

## Script structure (hook → pain → twist → solution → loop)

1. **Hook (0–3s):** a bold, surprising claim with a number. Example pattern: "[Number]. [Shocking benefit]."
2. **Pain (3–10s):** name the viewer's problem in plain words, with a concrete detail
   (e.g. "page 7 of Google") and a punchline ("Nobody goes to page 7.").
3. **Frustration (10–15s):** the costly or annoying status quo ("...and they charge you every single month.").
4. **Twist / solution (15–20s):** "So I [did X] instead." Then reveal the number and the system.
5. **Breakdown / loop (20s+):** introduce item #1 ("Agent 1: The Auditor, finds what's blocking you…").
   Ideally the ending loops back into the opening line so the reel replays seamlessly.

## Visual style

- Vertical 9:16, with a clean off-white/light-grey background and plenty of whitespace.
- **Kinetic typography:** words appear in sync with the voice.
  Mix a heavy bold sans-serif (headlines) with an *italic serif accent* for the key word or phrase
  (e.g. "₹0" bold + "*salary.*" italic serif, "6" big orange + "*AI agents.*").
- One strong accent colour (orange/red-orange) for key numbers and highlights. Everything else is neutral.
- Cute, rounded 3D mascot characters (simple robot heads with glowing eyes, colourful, each with a label)
  that represent the "team" or features. Original characters only.
- Simple UI mockups (browser window "yourwebsite.com", pagination 1–7, invoices stamped "DUE")
  to show the problem visually.
- Smooth pop/scale-in animations, a cursor clicking, and small character expressions (an angry face for the pain point).
- Small eyebrow labels above titles (e.g. "AGENT 1"), then a big bold title, then a one-line description.

## Post / caption rules

- Keep it **simple and attractive**: one idea per post, with very little text on screen.
- Caption: hook line first, 2–4 short lines, a clear CTA (e.g. comment a keyword to get a free playbook), and a few relevant hashtags.
- Always give each video its full voiceover script with timestamps alongside the on-screen text for each beat.
