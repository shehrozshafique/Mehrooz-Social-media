# Mehrooz Social Media: video and post style guide

Every video and post made in this repo follows this style unless the user says otherwise.
The user shared a reference reel **only as a style example**. Its topic, script, characters and wording are not to be reused.
Take only the *voice and the way it guides the viewer*, and the clean, simple look. The topic, script and visuals come fresh from each new request.

## Guiding voice (voiceover)

- **Every video has a guiding voiceover.** One narrator talks straight to the viewer and walks them through it step by step.
- Tone: calm, confident and conversational, like a friend explaining something useful. Not hype and not salesy.
- Use short, punchy sentences of 2 to 8 words, with a natural pause between lines.
- Speak to the viewer as "you"/"your". Keep it simple, with no jargon.
- Pace: about 2.5 words per second. A full reel runs 15 to 30 seconds.
- Use a clear, warm voice from a TTS or voice library. Do not clone or imitate any real person's voice.

## Script flow

1. **Hook (0–3s):** a bold, surprising line, ideally with a number.
2. **Problem (3–10s):** the viewer's problem in plain words, with one concrete detail and a short punchline.
3. **Why it hurts (10–15s):** what it's costing them.
4. **Solution (15–20s):** the fix or idea, stated simply.
5. **Guide / loop (20s+):** walk through step 1 (or the first point). Ideally the end flows back into the hook so the reel replays.

## Visual style

- Vertical 9:16, with a clean light background and plenty of whitespace.
- **Kinetic typography:** words appear in sync with the voice.
  Pair bold sans-serif headlines with an *italic serif accent* on the key word.
- One strong accent colour on key numbers and highlights. Everything else is neutral.
- Simple, friendly visuals that fit the topic (icons, mockups, original characters), chosen fresh for each video.
- Smooth pop/scale-in animations. Use small labels above titles (e.g. "STEP 1"), then a big title, then a one-line description.

## Post / caption rules

- Keep it **simple and attractive**: one idea per post, with very little text on screen.
- Caption: hook line first, 2–4 short lines, a clear call to action, and a few relevant hashtags.
- Always give each video its full voiceover script with timestamps alongside the on-screen text for each beat.

## Single-image posts (Mehrooz's brand)

- Brand: white and blue (#3461F5 → #1E2C7A). Size 1080×1350, exported at 2×.
- **No small label/bullet pill in the top-left** (e.g. "MY FREELANCE JOURNEY"). Don't add one unless asked.
- **Full-width, big headline** in a bold hero section. Put the hero number in a highlight.
- If the post is about a tool or brand (e.g. HighLevel), **feature its logo prominently** in the hero.
- Make it attractive: mix layout styles in one post (wide card, 2-column bento cards, one dark feature card)
  instead of identical rows. Give each point a colourful gradient icon tile plus a small visual that helps reading
  (chips, checklist, "X vs Y", mini quote).
- Comment-to-get posts: highlight the keyword in a comment-box mockup (avatar, typed keyword, send button).
- Footer on every post, in four parts: photo with **Muhammad Mehrooz Shafique** / CEO, Permanent SEO; services line;
  Repost; Permanent SEO logo with its name. Reuse the footer markup from `ghl-lessons-post/post.html`.
