More traffic ≠ more revenue

Organic traffic brings visitors. Not all of them are your ideal audience.

1
More traffic
  • Low intent
  • Irrelevant audience
  • High bounce rate
Example: a viral post that nobody buys from.
2
Qualified demand
  • Right audience
  • Higher engagement
  • More conversions
Example: a service page that answers one buyer's question.
